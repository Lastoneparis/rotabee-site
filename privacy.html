Privacy Policy — Rotabee

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Rotabee ("the app", "we") is a team shift and roster planner for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Apple Vision Pro (App Store) and for Android (Google Play). This policy explains what data the app handles. The short version: Rotabee is built to keep your data on your own devices. We do not run analytics, we do not show ads, and we do not track you. The sections below describe the Apple apps; Rotabee for Android explains where the Android app differs.

What we collect

Nothing is sent to us for analytics or advertising — we have no such systems. Rotabee has no accounts, no login, and no tracking SDKs.

The data you enter — team members' names, availability constraints, working rules and generated schedules — is stored locally on your device. If you enable iCloud, Apple syncs that data privately between your own devices through your personal iCloud account; we never see it and it is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Information that leaves your device

Rotabee only makes a network request in two cases:

  1. Purchases & subscriptions — handled entirely by Apple's App Store / StoreKit. Apple processes payment; we never receive your payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  2. Promo code redemption — if you enter a promo code, the app sends only the code itself to our verification endpoint (api.rotabee.app) to check validity and prevent reuse. No names, schedules, device identifiers or personal data are attached to that request.

How we use it

The data you enter is used solely to build and display your schedules on your device. We do not profile you, sell data, or share it with third parties.

Third-party services

  • Apple App Store / StoreKit — payments & subscription management. Policy
  • Apple iCloud (optional, only if you enable sync) — private device-to-device sync. Policy

Rotabee uses no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or social-login SDKs.

Data retention & deletion

Your data lives on your device. Delete the app to remove all local data. If iCloud sync was enabled, manage or delete that data in Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ iCloud ▸ Manage Storage on your Apple device. Redeemed promo codes are recorded as "used" on our server only as an opaque code value, with no link to you.

Rotabee for Android

  • Storage and backup. Your teams, members, constraints and schedules are stored in the app's private storage on your device. There is no iCloud sync on Android. If Android backup is turned on, Android includes this data in your device backup in your own Google account; that backup is managed by Google and we cannot access it.
  • Network. The Android app's own code makes no network requests. The only connection is Google Play's billing service. There is no promo-code endpoint on Android: "Redeem code" opens Google Play's redeem page.
  • Purchases & subscriptions. Processed by Google Play Billing. We never receive your payment details; the app only asks Google Play whether you own Premium. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. See the Google Privacy Policy. Google Play's billing library may send Google its own diagnostic logs to operate the service. Rating prompts use the Google Play In-App Review API.
  • Exports and calendar. When you export a PDF, CSV, ICS or image, or add a shift to your calendar, the file or event goes only to the app you choose, and you confirm the calendar entry yourself. Rotabee needs no calendar, contacts, location or storage permission.
  • No ads, no tracking. No accounts, ads, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and the advertising ID is not used. A small log of in-app events (such as "app opened" or "paywall shown") is kept on your device only and is never sent.
  • Deletion. Uninstall the app or clear its storage to remove all local data.

Children's privacy

Rotabee is rated 4+ and is a productivity tool. It does not knowingly collect any personal information from children, and it contains no user-generated public content, ads, or tracking.

Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because we do not collect or store personal data about you on our servers, there is no personal profile to access, correct, or delete. You remain in full control of the data on your device. For any question about this policy, contact us below.
